# Example Brief: Product Landing Page

Improve a product landing page so its audience, value proposition, proof, and next step are clear.

Focus on a specific headline, clear primary CTA and working destination, section order based on buyer questions, credible supplied proof, responsive layout, form feedback, image weight, and purposeful motion. Never fabricate logos, testimonials, or statistics.

Acceptance: the offer is quickly understood, narrow-screen use works, no CTA is dead, and build/browser checks are reported honestly.

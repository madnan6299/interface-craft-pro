# Landing Page Patterns

## Narrative sequence
A typical structure, adapted to the product:
1. Clear hero: who it is for, what outcome it delivers, primary call to action.
2. Credible proof or concrete product context; never invent testimonials or numbers.
3. Benefits tied to user outcomes, not a generic feature dump.
4. Product demonstration, workflow, or screenshots with useful context.
5. Objections, integrations, constraints, or FAQ where relevant.
6. Final next step with clear expectations.

Not every page needs every section. Choose sections based on the audience's decision process.

## Visual direction
Create a recognizable but restrained visual language. Use consistent typography, spacing, imagery, and component behavior. Avoid template-like repetition where every section is identical. Motion should support the story and remain skippable.

## Conversion and trust
- One primary CTA should dominate; secondary actions remain available but quieter.
- State what happens after clicking a CTA.
- Make pricing, eligibility, limitations, and contact details clear where relevant.
- Ensure forms are usable on mobile and have recoverable errors.
- Use authentic product visuals and accurate copy. Mark sample content as demo content.

## Responsive review
Check text wrapping, hero balance, CTA visibility, media cropping, navigation, and footer behavior on small screens. Test long titles and localized content, not just ideal sample copy.
